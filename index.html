About NDRT

We design, manufacture, test, and launch high-powered rockets. By working on complex systems—from custom avionics to experimental liquid propulsion—our members bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world engineering practice.

Learn About Us

Our Projects

We manage several concurrent engineering projects. Currently, our team is designing high-power solid vehicles for the NASA Student Launch (USLI) competition, while also developing Project NDXP—a multi-year initiative to build Notre Dame's first liquid bipropellant rocket engine.
